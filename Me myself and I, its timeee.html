Legal Research & Workflow Automation

Legal Research

How I Crack a Legal Matter

Every matter, whether a contract or legal advice, goes through the same three questions.

01

Identify the subject

Before looking at any law, I pin down what the matter is actually about.

02

Identify the governing law

Then I find which rules apply, and which of them prevails.

03

Identify the issues of concern

Finally I apply the law to the facts and look for what could go wrong.

The goal is not just to find the law, but to turn it into a clear answer the business can act on.
